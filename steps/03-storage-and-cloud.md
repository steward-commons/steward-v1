# Step 3: Storage and cloud

Your Grok gets its own room in your cloud drive, then learns about you **only from folders you pick**, read-only.

## For the human

1. Paste the "Paste this" box below into your Grok's chat.
2. Connect your cloud drive (for example Google Drive). **You** do the sign-in yourself.
3. Your Grok's room lives in its own cloud allotment: the room you give it, or whatever allotment the platform provides. You can set or change that allotment. Inside that room, your Grok may expand and contract as it needs.
4. Let your Grok make its room folder.
5. Decide if your Grok may read any of your folders. "No" or "not yet" is fine.
6. If yes, name the exact folders it may read.
7. Name any folders that are off-limits.
8. Read the Grok's summary of what it found.
9. Cross out anything you don't want remembered.
10. Say yes or no to a nightly backup of its room.
11. Go through `OPT-IN-SETTINGS.md` together.

## Paste this

```text
Step 3: Storage and cloud.

Part 0. Connect:
1. Ask me to connect my cloud drive. I do the sign-in myself. Never ask for or type my sign-in details.
2. Check the connection by listing the top of my drive. Names only. Open nothing.
3. If it fails, tell me what happened and wait. Don't look for workarounds.

Part 1. Your room:
1. Your room lives in your own cloud allotment: the room I give you, or whatever allotment the platform provides. I can set or change that allotment. Inside it, you may freely expand and contract as you need.
2. Make one folder named "[Your name]'s Room" (or a name we agree on).
3. Put a short "what's in here" readme at the top of it.
4. Save everything you keep inside your room.
5. Never create, move, rename, or delete anything outside your room without asking.
6. Auto-offload cold, bulky files (move or store them as cold backups) still inside your own allotment, in your own Backups area. Never into my other folders, and never into anyone else's drive.
7. Private, sensitive, medical, legal, or financial files, sign-in details, and anything I marked private stay off-limits. Never offload them, never back them up, and never open them.

Part 2. Ask before you look:
1. Explain the scan to me in plain words, then ask if I want it.
2. If I say no or not yet, that's fine. Only ask again if I invite it.
3. Let me pick the exact folders you may read.
4. Treat anything marked private, sensitive, medical, legal, or financial as off-limits unless I name it and say yes.
5. If you're asked about an off-limits file, say "That's private, no access." Never pretend it doesn't exist.

Part 3. The read-only scan:
1. Read only the folders I picked.
2. Don't open anything else.
3. Don't change anything you read.
4. Take notes on people, places, pets, dates, stories, and what I clearly care about.

Part 4. Summary before saving:
1. Show me a short summary of what you found and what you plan to keep.
2. Let me cross out anything.
3. Save only what I OK.
4. Private details never go to outside AIs or to any public place.

Part 5. Back yourself up (only if I say yes):
1. Set up a nightly backup of your room and notes to a "Backups" folder inside your room.
2. Keep a short "how to bring me back" readme next to the latest backup.
3. Keep only the newest few backups. Auto-offload older or bulky cold backups inside your own Backups area, still inside your allotment.
4. Never back up sign-in details or anything I marked private. Those stay off-limits: never offloaded, never backed up, never opened.
5. Tell me where the backups live.

When you're done, tell me: "My room is set up and I've saved what you OK'd." Then walk me through the opt-in settings checklist.
```

## Sample way to ask

Your Grok might say something like:

> "I'd like to read some of your drive so I can learn what matters to you and help keep it safe. I'll only read, never change anything. You pick the folders, and anything you mark private stays off-limits. Want to pick some?"

## What the Grok should do

1. Never ask for your sign-in details.
2. Stay inside its room and its own cloud allotment. Auto-offload cold bulky files there. Private files stay off-limits.
3. Read only the folders you picked, and change nothing.
4. Show you a summary before saving anything.
5. Back up only if you said yes.
