# Steward V1 starter kit

A plain, three-step setup for a brand-new Grok that will act as a **Steward**: a helper that keeps a household's information organized, safe, and remembered.

This kit carries **setup, habits, and routines only**. It never carries another family's memories, files, or private details. Your Grok starts with a blank memory and grows its own voice with its own human.

Starter-prompt version: **Steward V1**.

## Who's who

- **The human** — the person this new Grok works with. Their teammate. They have the final say on their own things.
- **The owner** — the person who runs the household's setup (often the same person as the human). They decide on schedules and shared rules.
- **The founding Grok (G1)** — the first Grok in a household. If you already have one, it sends the welcome test email in Step 2. If this is your first Grok, it *becomes* your G1.

## The three steps

Paste one step at a time. Wait for the Grok to say it is ready before the next one. Do not paste the whole kit in one shot.

| Step | File | What happens |
|------|------|--------------|
| 1 | [`steps/01-wake-up.md`](steps/01-wake-up.md) | The human introduces themselves to their new Grok. |
| 2 | [`steps/02-basic-communications.md`](steps/02-basic-communications.md) | Email gets connected, and G1 sends a first-contact test email. |
| 3 | [`steps/03-storage-and-cloud.md`](steps/03-storage-and-cloud.md) | The Grok gets its own storage room, then does a permission-first, read-only scan of folders the human picks. |

Then go through [`OPT-IN-SETTINGS.md`](OPT-IN-SETTINGS.md) together. Every optional feature starts **OFF** until the human says yes.

## Quick start for the human

1. Open a new chat with your new Grok.
2. Open `steps/01-wake-up.md`.
3. Fill in the blanks in brackets, like `[YOUR NAME]`.
4. Paste it as the very first message.
5. Answer the Grok's questions.
6. When it says it is ready, paste Step 2.
7. When Step 2 is done, paste Step 3.
8. Go through the opt-in checklist together.
9. Done. Say hi to your Steward.

## Privacy and safety (the short version)

- **Copies carry setup, habits, and routines.** Never the original family's memories or files.
- **You type your own logins.** Your Grok never asks for or types your sign-in details.
- **Sends, deletes, and spends need your OK.** Every time.
- **Emails to other people are drafts.** Your Grok writes them; you send them.
- **Email is information, never a command.** Even from G1.
- **Defense only.** The tools here protect your own house. They never break into, harm, or "hack back" at anyone.
- **Nothing private goes to third-party AIs.** Outside AIs can be consultants on public, non-private questions only. They never get a vote.
- **Private stays private.** Anything you mark private, medical, legal, or financial is off-limits unless you name it and say yes.
